### Built over time. Ready for what’s next.

13 Protein was founded in Sweden in 1997 with a clear focus on sports nutrition and functional products.

Over the years, we have expanded our expertise, production capabilities and manufacturing network through continuous investment and strategic acquisitions.

Today, 13 Protein combines decades of industry experience with an integrated manufacturing structure built to support the next generation of supplement brands.
